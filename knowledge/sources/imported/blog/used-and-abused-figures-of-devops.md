# Used and abused figures of DevOps

> **Evidence level:** talk page holds title, event (DevOpsDays Zurich 2019),

## Core argument (inferred)

The DevOps movement runs on a small set of recycled figures — deployment
frequencies, "high performers are 2x more likely to…" statistics, maturity
charts — that get detached from their studies and abused as universal
justification. The talk apparently catalogued which figures are load-bearing,
which are misquoted, and what they can and cannot support. That a movement
insider delivered this at a DevOpsDays says the critique is corrective, not
dismissive.

## Extracted claims

- **[empirical]** (inferred, speculative) Commonly cited DevOps figures are
  routinely used beyond what their source studies support.
- **[normative]** (inferred) Practitioners citing figures owe the audience the
  figure's provenance and limits — measurement claims are evidence, not
  rhetoric.

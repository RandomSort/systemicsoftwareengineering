# The work

Source piece: `bookofnull/pieces/042-the-work.md` (five-line litany).
Companion pieces: `011-slacking-off.md`, `010-sowhat.md`.

## Core argument

A litany of negations — the ticket, the meeting, the status update, even
prioritizing the work are not the work — ending in the tautology "The work
is the work". Coordination artifacts are representations of work, and
organizations drift toward optimizing the representations: tickets groomed,
boards immaculate, statuses current, while the thing the tickets are
*about* starves. The tautological ending is the point: the work cannot be
defined in terms of its proxies, only pointed at. Its companion (011)
complicates the boundary from the other side: thinking and resting look
like slacking off but may be the work, while visible busyness may not be.

## Extracted claims

- **Definitional:** tickets, meetings, status updates, and prioritization
  are maps of work; time spent on them is coordination overhead, justified
  only by the work it enables.
- **Empirical:** organizations tend to grow work-about-work as they scale,
  and measurement regimes reward legible proxy-work over illegible real
  work. (Checkable against studies of coordination overhead; Goodhart
  dynamics.)
- **Normative:** guard the ratio of work to work-about-work; when a proxy
  becomes the deliverable, stop and re-point at the work.
- **Normative (011):** do not equate visible activity with work; thinking
  is work that photographs as idleness.

## Framework relevance

The organizational instance of the corpus's map-vs-territory theme, and the
management-layer sibling of *The Green Build* (019): both are Goodhart
stories — one at the CI gate, one at the ticket board. Feeds concepts on
proxy metrics, legibility, and what "productivity" measures. A systemic
framing: every layer of coordination is a model of the layer below, and
each layer can be gamed independently.
